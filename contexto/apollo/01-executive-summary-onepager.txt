Most revenue teams operate as broken systems:

  ❌ List Fragmentation    → 20 reps, 20 lists, no single source of truth
  ❌ Campaign Chaos        → Overlapping campaigns with no shared learnings
  ❌ Data Scatter          → Signals live in 6 different tools
  ❌ Strategy Dilution     → What leadership asks for arrives at field unrecognizable

IMPACT: 35% longer sales cycle | 45% lower reply rates | 60% untouched market


THE SOLUTION: 7-Pillar GTM Architecture
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Apollo delivers a SYSTEM, not a tool. Each pillar builds on the last:

  1. BUILD TAM          → One qualified list per segment (10k-50k accounts)
  2. DELIVERABILITY     → Infrastructure math: safe email volume by market size
  3. SCORING            → Every account ranked by signals (fit + intent + timing)
  4. MESSAGING          → Same signal that scores triggers what you say to them
  5. WORKFLOWS          → Automated research, scoring, drafting across market
  6. HUMAN IN LOOP      → Top 10% of opportunities reviewed by experts
  7. REPORTING          → Signal correlation to replies/meetings drives optimization

KEY INNOVATION: One research effort, two outputs = Scoring + Messaging.
